# Lead-Nurture Email Sequence (2 emails: welcome → enrollment)

Triggered on the lead-magnet signup (see `lead-capture-path.md`). Two emails — short, high-signal, on-schedule — that convert a content subscriber into an enrollment application.

---

I run small, live mentorship cohorts (System Architecture, Agentic AI + MCP, Flutter) where working engineers like you get their actual production systems reviewed 1-on-1, in Bengali/Hindi/English.

>

>
> — Shibaji

**Purpose:** Deliver value instantly, establish authority (who I am / what I teach / proof), and open a two-way conversation. Low-commitment reply hook.

---

## Copywriting rules enforced across the sequence
- One clear CTA per email; no competing asks.
- Every claim maps to a visible proof point (live sessions, 1-on-1 review, real deployed system).
- Scarcity is honest (seat cap is real) — never fabricated.
- Bengali/Hindi/English delivery noted to match audience.
- Plain-text feel, short paragraphs, mobile-first.
